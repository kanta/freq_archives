f r e q
Kyushu Institute of Design Max Seminar Presents

Main/Theme/Date/Profile/Participation/FAQ/Link

f r e q is the place for tring pursuit of a relation between technology and music/image , and expression produced out of it.
This time, we focus on one of most powerful musical program environment "Max/MSP" and plug-in which adds the image processing & network environment to Max/MSP "nato.0+55+3d modular." We want to show their possibilities. Not to mention people making sound and the image, we wish those who like enjoying the sound itself, those whom an image likes, and those who are interested in the relation between technology and expression to come and join us.
The first day, the lecture of Max/MSP/nato will be held in the first half, and the live will be performed in the second half greeted guests from the electronic music scene of Fukuoka/Kyushu area. Powerful nato user portable[k]ommunity (p[k]) is greeted as main guests.
We want to provide with the opportunity of exploring the possibility of new music and image expression which does not remain in an academic field. Moreover, the workshop of Max/MSP/nato is planned in 2nd day. we are greeted p[k] as a lecturer.
We hope that appear the network which shares informations and ideas taking advantage of holding f r e q . Please contact us if you have interest.
