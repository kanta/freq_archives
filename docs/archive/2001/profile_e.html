portable[k]ommunity
The sound/image unit carried the ball by HORIKIRI Jun and SAWAI Taeji. They make full use of programing environments like a Max/MSP/nato.0+55... on the PowerBook. And are under portable activity. They are making CD now.
WebSite : http://www.portablekommunity.org/

HORIKIRI Jun : Living in Osaka. Free-lance profession. He studied philosophy at the university and the sound design at the IMI(Inter Medium Institute). And is taking charge of the lesson of sound edit and Max programming by IMI after graduation. Latest works are teaching Max/nato at Digital-Workshop hosted by salon magazine, writing for magazines, and so on.

SAWAI Taeji : Living in Nara. Ornament business. Although he has played musical instruments such as guitar, percussion, and synthesizer from the middle of the 10th generation, the main musical instrument was gradually shifted to digital equipments. And now, a work environment is completely shifted to PC-based. Recent works are the DSP-processed-improvisation accompanied by corporeality with e~da(a.k.a.AOA, ex. Boredoms), the work of the installation using pulse sound and electrodes with Mr. Fukuda of Musashino Art University, the live performance using ethnic musical instruments and a PowerBook at The World Festival of Sacred Music, Hiroshima 2001, and so on.

This seminar's activity has started from June, 2000 in wake of a lecture of FURUKAWA Kiyoshi(composer, assistant professor Tokyo National University of Fine Arts and Music, active at zkm and so on.). The activity began from the basic of MIDI, Max, MSP. Now, activities are creating works, criticizing each other, and what have you. And managed by students of K.I.D Acoustics Design Depertment. By the by, this seminar is authorised by Dr. Max Mathews (Max named after him!).

NAKAMURA Shigenobu
Nakamura composes various music ranging from contemporary music to music for television dramas and events. He has written more than 100 compositions in various genres, including 8 orchestral pieces and two symphonies.
Since the late 1980s, he has enthusiastically produced and presented Music Theater works using computers, and some of his Music Theater pieces using computers were accepted and presented at the International Computer Music Conference (ICMC) in 1992, 1995, 1996, and 1997.
Recently he has also produced visual works, which were accepted and presented at International Award for Video Art (Germany) in 1995 and 1996, "l'immagine leggera" Palermo International Videoart Film & Media Festival (Italy) in 1997 and 1999, and ICMC2001.
In 1997 and 1998 he was artist-in-residence at the ZKM Institute for Music and Acoustics.
He is presently professor in the Department of Acoustic Design at National Kyushu Institute of Design.
WebSite : http://www.tcn.zaq.ne.jp/s_nakamura/

　

KOBATA Ikuo
Living in Nagasaki, composer, teaching at Nagasaki Junshin Catholic University junior college part.
He is continuing composition activities as a free composer. And he have an interest in the music various period, and performs Gregorian chant, polyphony music of middle age, and modern pieces as a conductor of AuRA (AURA : female group). In recent years, he mainly performs the composition and the performance which used the computer, and is pursuing the possibility.
