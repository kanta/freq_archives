Robert Duckworth
new media artist robert duckworth (b. 1974) is currently pursuing a master of arts degree in composition new media under mark trayle at the california institute of the arts, where he served as a teaching assistant in the interactive electronic music studios. other teachers include tom erbe, wadada leo smith, morton subotnick, and james tenney. for the second year of his masters, he will undertake a one year directed field study of "japtop" music in tokyo under the auspices of calarts.
after becoming interested in pop-music, drumset & percussion as a teenager, duckworth studied music theory and music composition with lewis nielson and leonard v. ball, jr. at the university of georgia. during this time, he also studied japanese language and culture, graduating with a bmus in music composition.
since graduation, duckworth has studied at various seminars, workshops, and symposia around the world with such figures as pierre boulez (carnegie hall), julio estrada (ccmix), gerard pape (ccmix), miller puckette (suny), curtis roads (suny), karlheinz stockhausen (kurten, germany), david zicarelli (suny), and others.
website :: http://www.thing.net/~roddys/robert.html

Nao Tokui (clickety and clack, PROGRESSIVE FOrM)
Born in 1976 and resides in Tokyo. His production forte is to produce high quality, four-to-the floor which is tight and moreover groovy, with an element of speed. In 1998, he started DJing techno as well as producing minimal techno. In 2000, having seen the limit of production capacities of the combination of his own written C language software and AKAI MPC2000, he met up with the music programming software, Max/MSP.
In the following year, he fully started production with Max/MSP. At the same time, he started programming his own software within the Max/MSP. In the same year, he released his 1st 12" Single, "Nao Tokui&TNT/We All need" from the Belgian label, Pocket/Music Man. Also in 2002, from the Swiss label, Speaker Attack, he had a track included in their compilation, Deck Shark Series Vol. 4. This summer, as Clickery and Clack, he joins PROGRESSIVE FORM as their new artist.
And as a programmer studying at university, he does research on Artificial Life and Complex systems and is trying to apply Evolutionary Computation into music and design as well. Right now, waiting for the results of his research, he is building a visual programming software for music and film. This autumn, he is set to release a software program which is based on artificial intelligent-like Evolution and Emergence. He thinks that both "the act of producing music" as well as " the act of writing music programs" are similiar and it is "a way of self-expression". He would like to both utilize and combine "music production" and "software programming" and appeal both elements in the future.
website :: http://www.miv.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~tokui/

AOKI Takamasa(SILICOM, PROGRESSIVE FOrM)
Born in 1976 in Osaka. A new generation creator and composer who utilizes computer software. After graduating university, as nexus 6,5 starts his musical production. In Feb. 2000, changes his artist name to AOKI takamasa. By using cutting-edge technology and software, his original as well as experimental production approach, simaltaneously locks a groove and is very delicate and simple.AOKI takamasa's production attractiveness is that it fills the gap between computer/electronic club music and contemporary music which is both innovative and unique and this style "sounds like the computer itself alive and human, hitting the drum".
With visual/sound creator, TAKAGI masakatsu, AOKI takamasa produces and performs as SILICOM as well as at the same time, performs alone as well. Apart from his solo productions, he has participated in Yoshihiro Hanno's remix album, april remixes with oval/markus popp, christophe charles, and Takuji Aoyagi of little creatures.
He has been performing live last year for a total of over 40 times domestically and abroad (Japan, France, Turkey). Shigenobu NAKAMURA
Nakamura composes various music ranging from contemporary music to music for television dramas and events. He has written more than 100 compositions in various genres, including 8 orchestral pieces and two symphonies.
Since the late 1980s, he has enthusiastically produced and presented Music Theater works using computers, and some of his Music Theater pieces using computers were accepted and presented at the International Computer Music Conference (ICMC) in 1992, 1995, 1996, and 1997.
Recently he has also produced visual works, which were accepted and presented at International Award for Video Art (Germany) in 1995 and 1996, "l'immagine leggera" Palermo International Videoart Film & Media Festival (Italy) in 1997 and 1999, and ICMC2001.
In 1997 and 1998 he was artist-in-residence at the ZKM Institute for Music and Acoustics.
He is presently professor in the Department of Acoustic Design at National Kyushu Institute of Design.
website :: http://www.kyushu-id.ac.jp/~sn/
