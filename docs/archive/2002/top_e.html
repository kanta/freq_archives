FREQ is an electronic music workshops+lives performances+lecture over three days held at Fukuoka, Japan. Now, the electronic music is used in various areas and has various aspects. FREQ consists of concerts, workshops, and lectures of diverse styles, and you'll see a variety of scenes crossing each other, which originally evolved differently. This event will give you a good oppotunity to produce new ideas.
FREQ thinks practice is important, so workshops and lectures mainly constitute FREQ, which tries to achieve the mixture of each area. Therefore, for those who are creating music or using a computer for their creation, FREQ will be a great stimulus to your creation. FREQ invites you as a performer of live concert or a writer for a manuscript of the pamphlet. We are waiting for you to participate from a various field.
