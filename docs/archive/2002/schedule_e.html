Max/MSP lecture by Nao Tokui + Takamasa Aoki (SILICOM)

Max/MSP is known as music/image programming language. It is a very practical lecture for the upper users by two artists invited from PROGRESSIVE FOrM as lecturers. Following the examples of their patch, they will explain music works using Max/MSP and the application to the live performance.

15 : 15 - 16 : 15
venue：Kyushu Institute of Design Multi-Dimensional Hall
admission fee：free

Digital Analog Conversion lecture by Taeji Sawai (portable[k]ommunity)

When it has a look, it seems that the process of Digital Analog conversion is the same. but a big difference exists because of a difference in the algorithm between the software and in the circuit design between the hardware. This lecture comments on the difference in the quality of sound between the various software, hardware, and monitor speaker used for the actual music production.
