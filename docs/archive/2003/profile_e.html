  • Studied at the Michaelis School of Art up to 1970, and awarded a DAAD Scholarship to study a further three years from 1971 at the Hamburg College of Art. Worked as an independant filmmaker from 1973 on, and released some abstract animations based on the electronic music, after a commission from ZDF (German TV). His staying in Korea allowed him to produce "THE KOREAN TRILOGY" from 1984 to 1990. Each piece of the triology "LUNG", "FENG HUANG" and "STONE LION" is all realised as abstract visual work which stands on musical concepts. His first computer animation, arrived from 1991 until 1993, was entitled "MEMB". And from 1993 to 1996, arrived a further experimental animation "MOE'S FIELD". Parallelly to this, he designed an interactive virtual reality sequence for an interactive short opera by Kiyoshi Furukawa “Die Ungeborenen Goetter” for the sake of the ZKM (CENTRE FOR ART AND MEDIA TECHNOLOGY) in Karlsruhe. Worked on a further development of the collage techniques from 1996 to 1999, in a animation project "STELE", a Video-Animation series of NOEMATA No.1” and “NOEMATA No.2” follows. Engaged as visiting professor in the Inter-Media Art Department of the Tokyo National University of Fine Arts and Music from 2001 to 2002, and from 2002 on, he is teaching professor at the Nagoya University of Arts and Sciences.
