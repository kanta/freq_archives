    Lecture and Video show　"Audio and visual arts: its history and current situation"　by Robert DARROLL
      Examinates the situation and the future of multimedia (multi-modal) expression through introduction of many image works including some materials rarely presented even in Europe. * Robert DARROLL is a multi modal artist. After the teaching career as guest professor at Tokyo National University of Fine Arts and Music, he now is a professor of Nagoya University of Arts and Science.


    Lecture and Presentation "Artistic and design-oriented education for multi-modal expression"
