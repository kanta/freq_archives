  • The 'freq' is an annual event series including comprehensively: performance, show, and lecture of various digital arts, but especially conceived on sound dimension. It is held form 2001, as a place where the artists investigate new artistic expressions occuring by sound and an image, and information technology. A spread network is shown towards the world, which shares informations and ideas.

    In this "freq03", not only presenting new multimedia work, but also educational succession towards the future will be carried out, while two fields, artistic activities and engineering research, are examinated historically as deeply involved each other. We do prepare to make this two-days event for experimental arts enjoyable and intellectual one.
